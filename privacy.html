Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZeroUp Developers ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use any of our mobile applications published on Google Play (the "Apps"). It applies to every application listed on our apps page.

In short: we do not sell your personal data. We collect the minimum needed to run the Apps, keep your learning progress, and show advertising that keeps the content free.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details — if you sign in, we store your display name, email address and profile photo as supplied by your chosen sign-in provider.
  • Feedback — any message you send us through the in-app feedback form.

Information collected automatically

  • Learning progress — completed lessons, quiz scores, streaks and leaderboard position.
  • Device and usage data — device model, operating system version, app version, language setting, crash reports and general usage events.
  • Advertising identifiers — an advertising ID used to deliver and measure ads.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone input, or any special-category personal data.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Apps and save your progress across sessions and devices.
  • To display leaderboards and league standings where the App offers them.
  • To show advertising, including rewarded ads you choose to watch.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix defects and improve lesson content.
  • To send optional notifications, such as lesson reminders. You can turn these off at any time in your device settings.

4. Advertising and your choices

Advertising keeps our learning content free. Ads are shown as banners, interstitials, native placements and rewarded videos. Rewarded videos are always optional — you choose whether to watch one in exchange for an in-app benefit.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of personalised advertising, from your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

Before any ad is requested, our Apps present a consent message through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a certified Consent Management Platform. You may accept or decline personalised advertising, and you can change your choice later from the App's settings screen. If consent is declined, we serve non-personalised advertising only.

5. Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR applies, we process personal data on the following bases: performance of a contract (providing the App you requested), consent (personalised advertising and optional notifications), and legitimate interests (security, crash diagnostics and product improvement).

6. Data retention

Account and progress data is retained while your account remains active. If you delete your account, associated data is removed from our production database within 30 days. Aggregated, non-identifying analytics may be retained for longer.

7. Your rights

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw advertising consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email zeroupdevelopers@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure with access restricted to the studio. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers

Our service providers operate globally, so your data may be processed on servers outside your country of residence, including in the United States, under the safeguards those providers maintain.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
